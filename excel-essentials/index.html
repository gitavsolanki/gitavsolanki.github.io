Lesson 2

✏️ Entering & Formatting Data

Enter words and numbers into cells, and make your spreadsheet easier to read.

  1. Click a cell and type a label, such as Product.
  2. Press Enter and type a number in the next cell.
  3. Use the Home tab to make headings bold or format money as currency.
  4. Adjust column widths if your text is cut off.

Lesson 3

➕ Formulas & SUM

Use Excel to calculate totals instead of adding numbers by hand.

Formulas begin with an equals sign. To add values in cells B2 to B6, type =SUM(B2:B6) into an empty cell and press Enter.

Text alternative: Select the total cell, enter =SUM(B2:B6), then press Enter.

Lesson 4

🖱️ Using the Fill Handle

The Fill Handle helps you copy formulas and continue patterns quickly.

Click a cell. Move your pointer to the small square at its bottom-right corner, then drag across or down.

Text alternative: Select a cell, point to its bottom-right corner and drag to copy or fill.
